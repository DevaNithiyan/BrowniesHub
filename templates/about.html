Our Heritage

Artisanal Baking Rooted in Passion & Uncompromising Quality

Started as a humble family kitchen in Bengaluru, Brownies Hub & Sweet Crumbs was built on a simple philosophy: never cut corners on quality. We believe that truly memorable baked goods come from patience, craft, and the finest natural ingredients.

Every single morning at 5:00 AM, our bakers begin kneading stone-ground flour, whipping fresh dairy cream, and tempering authentic Belgian chocolate. No margarine, no artificial preservatives, no shortcuts — just pure baked happiness.

Why Choose Us

The Four Pillars of Sweet Crumbs

What makes our brownies, cakes, pastries, and breads taste truly exceptional.

🧈

100% Farm Dairy Butter

We never use hydrogenated oils or margarine. Every croissant and cake is laminated with real dairy butter for unmatched flakiness.

🍫

Belgian Couverture Chocolate

Our decadent brownies, cookies, and chocolate cakes use single-origin Belgian cocoa and rich cacao butter for a melt-in-mouth experience.

🌾

24-Hour Sourdough Ferment

Our sourdough loaves and artisan baguettes undergo slow 24-hour natural wild fermentation for easier digestion and incredible depth of flavor.

🚚

Daily Fresh Oven Delivery

We bake in small daily batches so you receive bread and pastries freshly out of the oven, delivered warm to your doorstep.
