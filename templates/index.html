Our Story

Artisanal Baking Rooted in Passion & Quality

Started as a humble family kitchen in Bengaluru, Brownies Hub & Sweet Crumbs was built on a simple philosophy: never compromise on quality. Every cake, croissant, loaf, and cookie is slow-baked using traditional European techniques, pure dairy butter, and no artificial preservatives.

🧈 Pure Butter & Cream

No margarine or artificial oils. Only 100% farm-fresh dairy butter.

🌾 Slow Fermentation

Our sourdoughs and breads undergo 24-hour slow natural fermentation.

🍫 Belgian Chocolate

We source authentic couverture chocolate for our truffles and brownies.

🚚 Fresh Local Delivery

Packaged with care and delivered swiftly across the city.
